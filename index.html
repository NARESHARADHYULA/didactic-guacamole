Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this resource designed for?

HemeOnc Questions is designed for hematology/oncology fellows preparing for their boards, residents studying for in-service exams, and practicing clinicians who want to stay current. It is also useful for internal medicine physicians who want to strengthen their hematology and oncology knowledge.

How are the questions structured?

Questions are written in a board-style clinical vignette format with four answer choices. Each question is followed by a concise explanation covering the correct answer and key teaching points. Questions are tagged by category (benign hematology, malignant hematology, solid tumors) and difficulty level.

How often is the content updated?

The question bank is updated regularly to reflect new FDA approvals, updated NCCN guidelines, ASH/ASCO consensus statements, and landmark clinical trial results. Subscribers always have access to the most current content.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes! You can access a selection of sample questions without creating an account. To access the full question bank, choose one of our membership plans above. We do not offer refunds, so we encourage you to try the sample questions first.

Does my subscription include the mobile app?

Yes. Every paid subscription includes access to our iOS and Android mobile apps at no extra charge. Simply download the app and log in with your account credentials.

Can I access the site on multiple devices?

Yes. Your account can be used on any web browser as well as our mobile apps. Your progress and bookmarks sync across all devices automatically.

What resources do you recommend using alongside this?

We recommend using HemeOnc Questions alongside ASCO-SEP, ASH-SAP, and NCCN guidelines for comprehensive board preparation. Our resource is designed to complement these materials with focused, high-yield question practice.

About the Author

HemeOnc Questions was created by Dr. Ravin Garg, a board-certified hematologist-oncologist in academic practice. Recognizing the need for a focused, high-yield question bank that could fit the demanding schedule of a busy fellow, Dr. Garg wrote and curated all 2,600+ questions and explanations.

The resource launched in 2018 and has since grown to more than 15,000 registered users worldwide, spanning fellows, attending physicians, international medical graduates, and clinical pharmacists seeking specialty knowledge.

Dr. Garg reviews and updates the content continuously, ensuring that every subscriber has access to current, clinically relevant material aligned with the latest board examination blueprints.
